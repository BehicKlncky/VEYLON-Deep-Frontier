# Urban Survival — Decisions

Decision log, compatibility policy and open owner questions for the roadmap in [DESIGN.md](DESIGN.md). Recorded by milestone 01 on 2026-10-07 against `923345f`. Milestone 02 added D-32 to D-38 and the notes marked **02** in C-1, C-2, §3 and P-05, the same day. Milestone 03 added D-39 to D-47 and the notes marked **03** on 2026-10-08. Milestone 04 added D-48 to D-56 and the notes marked **04**, also on 2026-10-08. `B-nn` ids refer to verified facts in [VALIDATION.md §2](VALIDATION.md#2-verified-baseline-at-923345f).

**Status vocabulary.**
- **Adopted**: in force. The owner may steer it later.
- **Owner**: a product decision for the owner. The stated default applies until answered.
- **Package NN**: an engineering choice delegated to that package within the stated constraints. The package records its choice here.
- **Superseded**: replaced by a later decision, which is named.

| D-33 | **Revision mechanics (implements D-04).** *Layout*: one urban generator per layout revision. `UrbanRevision.layoutRevisionOf` is an exact switch: generator 4 builds layout revision 1, the street-datum placeholder (stone at y 0–39, a stone-brick street at y 40, air above, soil moisture from the climate biome). Content therefore can never be rolled against a revision a save is about to replace. A save refuses to write an unresolved revision. *Retirement*: a layout revision is retired by removing its generator from the supported set and adding a specific rejection message; a content revision by raising `MIN_SUPPORTED_CONTENT`. | D-40 | **City plan model (implements D-06 and D-07; adjustments recorded here).** Pure per-region plans (`CityPlanner`, 256 × 256 blocks). Avenues on every region edge: 12 wide, 3 sidewalk + 6 roadway + 3 sidewalk, mirrored halves; each segment between two region corners is plain (dashed centre line, 70 %) or a boulevard (2-cell planted median, 30 %), decided by (axis, edge, segment) so both regions agree. Three local streets per axis, 8 wide (2 + 4 + 2), at 64·k ± 5 blocks; a north-south street's position depends on (seed, region column, k) only and an east-west street's on (seed, region row, k) only, so every street runs straight through the avenues, every crossing is a full intersection on both sides of a seam, and the lattice is connected everywhere. Superblocks are therefore 46–66 blocks a side. Kinds: **courtyard housing** (six parcels: two strips of two around a 3-wide passage each, one parcel per side; strip depths 12–18; a courtyard of at least 8 × 8 with a 2-wide walkway ring onto which every rear entrance opens), **shop row** (two rows of 2–3 shop parcels back to back along a 3-wide through alley), **park** (lawn, a cross of paths to all four streets, a central square, trees) and **plaza** (paved, planted trees). Residential regions: courtyard housing, a park in 60 % and a shop row in 50 % of regions; corner parcels are corner shops at 20 %. Commercial regions (35 % of non-starter regions): shop rows, four courtyard blocks, a plaza in 60 %; corner shops at 40 %. At most one park or plaza per region. One parcel per region is a service reservation. Region (0, 0) is residential; its superblock 5 is courtyard housing whose west side parcel is the starter refuge and whose north-east corner parcel is a shop. **Adjustments to DESIGN §4.2:** parcels per superblock are exactly 6 (courtyard) or 4–6 (shop row); the parcel index is `superblock * 8 + slot`, so an index never shifts when another superblock holds fewer parcels; alleys run only through shop rows, and courtyards are reached through passages; no canal exists yet, because no flooded district is planned before 13; the ground is flat at the datum (y 40), so elevations agree by construction. ## 5. Change procedure

1. Mandatory owner requirements R1–R10 (DESIGN §1) change only on an explicit owner instruction, recorded here with its date. Difficulty, time or scope pressure is never a reason to drop one; the gate stays pending instead.
2. Adopted decisions change through a new row that supersedes the old one (`Superseded by D-nn`), with the reason and the affected packages and records.
3. Package decisions are filled in by the package that makes them, in the same change as the code. The handoff in PROGRESS.md names them.
4. Any change to persistence, enums or generation also follows `.agents/skills/veylon-save-compatibility/SKILL.md`.
