# VEYLON: Deep Frontier 0.6.0 — Signal & Silence

Version 0.6.0 rebuilds the sound of the frontier: layered weather, positioned
fire, environmental acoustics, varied physical actions, live volume controls and
short musical responses separated by long periods of silence. Every sound and
musical phrase is synthesized by project code. Existing worlds and saves retain
the same gameplay and terrain.

## Highlights

- Rain and wind use longer continuous layers with wide, independently generated
  emitters. Irregular droplets, crackles, drips and insect calls are scheduled at
  runtime, and weather intensity changes timbre.
- Fire has a physical position. Optional OpenAL EFX provides six interpolated
  reverb environments, wall muffling, air absorption and filtered indoor rain.
- Thunder retains the lightning position, waits for travel time and loses level
  and high frequencies with distance. Loading clears pending old-world thunder.
- Eighteen frequent sounds have four distinct takes. Twenty-four one-shot voices
  preserve important reports and alerts under pressure, with a short release
  before stealing an occupied source.
- Six synthesized 12-second music phrases respond to scene state. After a phrase,
  the director leaves 120-180 seconds of silence; the first night is a one-session
  event. Music uses its own source and stays beneath gameplay cues.

## Controls

Choose **AUDIO** on the title screen or press **V** on the title/pause menu.
Drag the four sliders or use arrow keys; toggle mute to silence every bus while
remembering the levels. **Apply/F5** persists preferences; **Back/Escape** restores
the previous mix. Setting music to zero fades the current phrase out and prevents
new music. `veylon_audio.properties` lives beside graphics preferences.

## Quality and measured limits

The final catalog has 122 mono buffers at 44.1 kHz, totaling 40,824,424 signed-16
PCM bytes. A 64 MiB payload ceiling and 1,500 ms synthesis budget are tested;
separate CPU budgets cover bounded ray sampling and music scheduling. All 486
tests in 79 classes pass in two consecutive reruns, alongside the full build,
JavaDoc doclint, benchmarks and Windows packaging. Both 30-second native smoke
runs (EFX and forced dry) pass all hard limits with zero OpenAL/GL/KHR errors.
Complete evidence is recorded in
[the validation report](../engineering/v0.6.0-validation.md).

Signal tests prove clean edges, DC/headroom, durations and selected frequency
bands. Scheduler tests prove bounds and over 90% music silence. Native runs prove
playback and resource submission on the tested device. Human judgments about
musical balance, perceived reverb scale, repetition fatigue, stolen-voice clicks
and recognizing a loop period over 60 seconds remain unverified.

## Compatibility

Save format v3, generator version 3 and persisted enum order are unchanged.
World, save, entity, combat and AI implementation files are unchanged. Lightning
retains its original simulation RNG calls and gameplay effects. Presentation
randomness remains outside simulation reseeding. No audio assets, dependencies,
background audio thread or decoder were added. Missing EFX uses dry playback;
a missing sound device disables audio safely.
