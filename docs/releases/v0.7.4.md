# VEYLON 0.7.4 — Articulated Ragdolls

Dying bodies are now fully jointed. Elbows, knees and hocks bend, heads and tails
swing on their own joints and bird wings flop, so a body folds, catches and
drapes over terrain instead of falling as one stiff slab.

## What changed

- **Bodies that fold.** People have ten joints: neck, head, upper arms,
  forearms, thighs and shins. Four-legged animals, hares included, have twelve:
  upper and lower legs, neck, head and a two-link tail, with front knees and rear
  hocks bending opposite ways. A bird's head, tail and four wing joints all move.
  Every joint is a hinge or a cone with its own limits.
- **Contacts decide how a body lies.** The spring that rolled every body onto a
  preset side is gone. Gravity, limb and torso contact with the ground and
  friction decide the landing, so a body killed at a ledge can keep its legs on
  the shelf while its upper body and arm hang over the edge.
- **Still deterministic.** The same kill produces the same fall at 30 and
  144 fps, and every body still settles within six seconds.
- **Poses survive saves.** A settled carcass or corpse keeps its full joint pose
  through save and load. Bodies saved by 0.7.2 or 0.7.3 still load, with their
  new lower joints straight.
- **Living creatures look the same.** Living models and animations are
  pixel-identical to 0.7.3; the new joints only bend after death.

Death consequences, harvesting, lodged-arrow recovery and blood effects work as
they did. With the maximum twelve bodies in flight, articulation costs about
0.36 ms per physics step on the test machine, about 2% of a 60 fps frame, and
the solver still allocates nothing per step.

The save format stays binary v3, and existing saves remain compatible. Body
poses now use version 2 of the optional `world.bodies` section. **0.7.2 and
0.7.3 cannot open a save written by 0.7.4**: they report "No compatible save was
found." The file is not modified, and 0.7.4 still opens it.

## Validation

The Windows Java 25 release build passed all 775 tests across 109 classes with
no failures or skipped tests, including Javadoc and Windows packaging
(`gradlew.bat build releaseArtifacts --rerun-tasks --no-daemon --console=plain`,
2026-09-18). The local archive was checked for its executable, bundled runtime,
versioned JAR and required launcher options.

Headless tests hold joint limits and segment lengths through complete falls and
check visible elbow and knee folding, terrain clearance for every species,
identical results at 30 and 144 fps, the six-second backstop, Creative and
Survival parity, version 1 save migration and zero allocation per step.

Native before/after captures at 1600×900 cover close human, quadruped,
ledge-drape and living-model scenes, with zero GL and KHR errors. The living
scenes show zero changed pixels. Native graphics were validated on Windows x64
only.

`performanceTest` ran on a secondary machine, not the reference machine. The new
ragdoll budget passed at 0.372 ms against 1.0 ms. Every other measured budget
passed except durable save, which already failed on that machine before this
change (6.11 ms before, 5.75 ms after, against 2.20 ms); its failure stops the
test before the load timing. The save gate should be investigated separately on
the reference machine.

The torso is still one rigid box. There are no finger, foot or spine joints, and
limbs do not collide with each other or with other bodies. See the
[validation record](https://github.com/BehicKlncky/VEYLON-Deep-Frontier/blob/v0.7.4/docs/engineering/RAGDOLL_VALIDATION.md)
for captures, commands and measurements, and
[joint authoring](https://github.com/BehicKlncky/VEYLON-Deep-Frontier/blob/v0.7.4/docs/engineering/RAGDOLL_JOINTS.md)
for the skeleton format and tuning.

The tag workflow builds and validates Windows x64, macOS Apple Silicon and
macOS Intel archives, then publishes them with `SHA256SUMS.txt`. Each archive
includes Java; a separate Java installation is not required. macOS builds are
unsigned and not notarized.
