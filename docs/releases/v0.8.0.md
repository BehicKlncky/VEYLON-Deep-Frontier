# VEYLON 0.8.0 — Lethal Combat & Molotov Fire

Being shot now means something. A bullet or an arrow in the head kills outright,
a chest takes two bullets or three arrows whoever it belongs to, a bomb leaves
nobody inside its lethal radius alive or whole, and the fire bomb has become a
molotov that spills burning liquid across the ground.

## What changed

- **Where you hit a person decides the fight.** A bullet or an arrow in the head
  kills outright, and a chest takes two bullets or three arrows whoever they are
  and however much health they have. An arrow wound counts for less than a
  bullet's, wounds from both add up, and a limb still costs only the weapon's own
  damage. Every pellet of one blunderbuss shot counts as a single chest wound.
  Shots NPCs fire at each other follow the same rules; the player and animals are
  unchanged.
- **Blasts leave nobody whole.** Anyone caught inside a scrap bomb's lethal
  radius of 3.9 blocks, or a powder keg's 5.7, dies whatever their health and
  whatever cover they are behind, and is torn into ten pieces — torso, head,
  upper arms, forearms, thighs and shins. The pieces fly, bounce off walls, come
  to rest where the terrain puts them and rot away like corpses. Further out,
  blasts deal the damage they always did, and a death by any other cause still
  leaves a whole body.
- **Fire bombs are molotovs.** A bottle breaks on the first thing it hits instead
  of waiting on a fuse, makes no explosion at all, and spills a pool of burning
  yellow-orange liquid that runs downhill and around obstacles. It burns whoever
  stands in it, sets light to grass, trees and buildings it touches, and lights
  the fuse of a powder keg beside it. Blocks it lights then spread like any other
  fire.
- **Fire climbs.** A spread roll picks fuel rather than any neighbouring cell and
  favours the layer above, so fire now carries reliably up a bare trunk into a
  canopy or along a wooden wall. A fire at the foot of a five-log trunk under a
  two-deep crown reached the crown in 0 of 200 seeds before this change and 200
  of 200 after.
- **Rain puts fires out.** Rain, storm and snow extinguish any fire open to the
  sky within a couple of seconds and leave its block unburnt — a log stays a log
  — while fires under a roof or a tree's crown burn on at a reduced rate. Burning
  pools soak out the same way, and a lightning fire in a storm flares and goes
  out.
- **Scenes for all of it.** Six capture scenes ship with the build:
  `dismember_showcase`, `dismember_wall` and `dismember_bomb`, which throws a
  real scrap bomb into a group, plus `molotov_ground`, `molotov_tree` and
  `molotov_rain`.

Harvesting, looting, reputation, quest credit and every other death consequence
fire exactly as they did: a person killed by the new rules dies an ordinary
death. Creatures and the player keep the damage model they always had and are
never dismembered. At the worst case the game allows — twelve people blown apart
into 120 pieces, seven burning pools and a full 220 block fires stepped together
at 60 fps — every hard limit held on every frame and the fragment step allocated
24 bytes per frame.

The save format stays binary v3, and existing saves remain compatible. Settled
body pieces go into a new optional `world.fragments` section rather than a new
version of an existing one, so **older builds still open a save written by
0.8.0**: they skip the section they do not know and lose only the pieces. Pools
of burning liquid and burning blocks are not saved, as burning blocks never
were; a bottle still in the air is saved with the other explosives and breaks
where it lands.

## Validation

The Windows Java 25 release build passed all 886 tests across 121 classes with
no failures or skipped tests, including Javadoc and Windows packaging
(`gradlew.bat build releaseArtifacts --rerun-tasks --no-daemon --console=plain`,
2026-09-20). The local archive was checked for its executable, bundled runtime,
versioned JAR and required launcher options.

Headless tests hold the hit-zone thresholds and the wound table for both
projectiles, blunderbuss pellets counting once, the lethal radius and cover
failing inside it, ten pieces instead of a ragdoll, fragment collision,
determinism, caps and decay, the new save section through a round trip and
against corrupt payloads, rain extinguishing without consuming the block, spread
up a trunk and along a wall, the pool's shape and bounds, entity burns and
Creative invulnerability, keg chains, and the three rules stepped together.

Native captures at 1280×720 cover all six scenes with zero GL and KHR errors.
The release smoke run passed: save and load both true, 55 materials, 656 glyphs,
110 of 110 item icons, zero GL and KHR errors, every new collection empty at the
end, and 142.5 average FPS with a p99 of 8.25 ms against the mandatory 60.
Native graphics were validated on Windows x64 only.

`performanceTest` ran on a secondary machine, not the reference machine, and 8
of 9 gates pass. A full field of twelve ragdolls costs 0.382 ms per fixed tick,
the whole simulation cycle 0.149 ms and the medium bucket including fire
0.020 ms. The durable-save gate fails at 5.977 ms against its 2.20 ms budget, as
it already did on that machine before this change; it should be investigated
separately on the reference machine.

Torsos and heads barely move at scrap-bomb strength, because mass follows volume
and only limbs take enough impulse to fly far. A save taken mid-burn loads with
the fires out. Hit-zone thresholds are heights read off the one humanoid model
every archetype uses. Wounds live on the live person, so a save and load returns
them at their stored health with none. Only people are dismembered, and pieces
cannot be harvested or looted. See the
[engineering record](https://github.com/BehicKlncky/VEYLON-Deep-Frontier/blob/v0.8.0/docs/engineering/COMBAT_LETHALITY_AND_MOLOTOV.md)
for every constant behind these rules, the full test inventory, the capture
commands and the rest of the deliberate limits.

The tag workflow builds and validates Windows x64, macOS Apple Silicon and
macOS Intel archives, then publishes them with `SHA256SUMS.txt`. Each archive
includes Java; a separate Java installation is not required. macOS builds are
unsigned and not notarized.
