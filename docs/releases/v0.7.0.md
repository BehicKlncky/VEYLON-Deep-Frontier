# VEYLON: Deep Frontier 0.7.0 — Stone & Sky

Version 0.7.0 adds a **Creative mode** to the frontier. Choose it when you start
a new world, or switch an existing world from the pause menu. In Creative you
cannot be harmed and have no needs, wildlife and settlers ignore you, you fly,
you build without cost from a catalog of every item, and you decide what time it
is and what the sky is doing. Survival plays exactly as it did in 0.6.0 — same
rules, same RNG draw order, same outcome for the same seed — and every existing
save keeps working.

## Highlights

- **Two game modes.** `NEW FRONTIER` opens a mode choice; Survival is the
  default. Terrain, the camp, the starter kit and the first wildlife are
  identical for the same seed in either mode.
- **Switching an existing world** is deliberate and visible. `G` while paused
  opens a confirmation, and the first switch to Creative states plainly that the
  world will be **permanently marked** as a Creative world. The mark never
  clears and is shown in the pause header and on the victory screen. Nothing in
  gameplay can switch modes by accident.
- **An invulnerable body.** Nothing hurts a Creative player: creatures, humans,
  projectiles, explosions, fire, falls, starvation, thirst, cold, heat,
  afflictions, smoke, toxic fog or bad food. No hunger, thirst, stamina cost,
  fatigue, carry weight or medical pressure. The HUD replaces the survival bars
  with a Creative badge and keeps the environment readouts.
- **Nothing notices you.** Wildlife and settlers never detect, pursue, flee from,
  charge, aim at or attack a Creative player, and mining or gunfire beside a
  settlement raises no alarm. The frontier keeps living regardless: chunks
  stream, settlements work, quests progress, and reputation, ownership, theft
  and vandalism still count against you.
- **Flight.** Double-tap `Space` to take off or land, hold `Space` to rise,
  `Ctrl` to descend and `Shift` to fly faster. Collision is kept — there is no
  noclip — and you cannot fly out of the generated world. Returning to Survival
  ends the flight and falls hurt again from that height.
- **A searchable catalog.** `E` in Creative opens every item by category or by
  name. Left click takes a full stack, right click one item, and `1`–`9` fills a
  hotbar slot. The inventory tab adds a trash slot.
- **Instant building.** Blocks break the moment you press, and every 0.30 s while
  you hold, with no tool requirement, no drops and no tool wear. Placing never
  spends the stack. The middle mouse button picks the block you are looking at.
- **Nothing runs out.** Bows fire without arrows, firearms reload without
  reserve ammunition, bombs, food, drinks and medicine are not used up, and
  carried items neither wear nor spoil.
- **A builder's palette.** Nineteen blocks that the world could show but you
  could never place — grass, leaves, ice, the five ores, basalt, stone brick,
  ruin stone, pod hull, wreckage, bone piles, charred blocks, cage bars, bushes,
  tall grass and glow fungus — now have Creative-only item forms.
- **World controls.** `T` while paused sets the time of day (forward only),
  freezes the daylight cycle, chooses and locks the weather, and pauses wildlife
  spawning. Returning to Survival releases all of them.

## Controls

| Key | Action |
| --- | --- |
| `G` (paused) | Game mode — switch this world between Survival and Creative |
| `T` (paused, Creative) | World controls — time, daylight freeze, weather and its lock, spawning |
| `Space` twice (Creative) | Start or stop flying |
| `Space` / `Ctrl` / `Shift` (flying) | Rise / descend / fly faster |
| `E` (Creative) | Creative catalog; `/` focuses search, `1`–`9` fill a hotbar slot |
| `LMB` / `RMB` (Creative) | Break instantly / place without spending the stack |
| `MMB` (Creative) | Pick the block you are looking at |

## Quality and measured limits

- 638 tests across 97 classes, zero failures, errors and skips, with JavaDoc
  doclint in the ordinary `build`.
- Every Creative behaviour is pinned together with its Survival twin from the
  same fixture, so a gate that accidentally fires in Survival fails a test.
- Every performance budget is unchanged from 0.6.0 and met with margin.
- Creative and Survival 30-second native smokes both pass with zero GL,
  KHR-debug and OpenAL errors.
- Creative adds no thread, no asset, no dependency and no outcome-affecting
  randomness.

## Compatibility

- The save format stays **binary v3**. Mode, the permanent mark, flight and the
  world controls live in two new optional sections; a save without them loads as
  an unmarked Survival world, so every save from 0.3.0–0.6.11 keeps working, and
  v2 saves (0.1.0–0.2.0) still migrate as before.
- A malformed new section fails the whole load rather than loading partial
  state, and the live world survives the attempt.
- **Older builds** skip the new sections: they open a 0.7.0 Creative save as
  Survival, and saving over it there discards the mark, flight and the world
  controls. A save holding the new Creative-only block items also loads on an
  older build, but those stacks are silently dropped; blocks already placed in
  the world survive, because no block id changed.
- One save slot, shared by both modes.
