# VEYLON 0.8.1 — Living Bodies & Fire

Lethal blasts now tear every living body apart, including animals and the
Survival player. Fire clings to bodies after they leave the flames, sends people
and animals fleeing, and follows a dying body onto its remains.

## What changed

- **Every body has its own pieces.** Scrap bombs and powder kegs kill every
  living body inside their lethal radius, including birds in flight. People
  separate into ten pieces, deer into eleven, wolves, thornhorns and gloomstalkers
  into twelve, and hares and birds into seven. Pieces fly, collide, settle and
  decay. An animal's torso keeps its meat, hide and lodged arrows for one harvest.
- **Bodies keep burning.** Burning liquid, burning blocks, fueled campfires,
  placed torches and direct fire-bomb hits ignite living bodies. Overlapping
  flames act as one fire. Deep water and open rain extinguish it; a roof shelters
  it. The Survival player can catch fire and develops a burn injury after a
  second alight; that injury starts hurting after the flames go out.
- **Fire interrupts daily life.** Burning people and animals stop fighting,
  working, trading, sleeping or talking and flee in erratic bursts. Birds flee
  in flight. They resume their ordinary decisions after recovering. Player
  controls remain available, and Creative remains invulnerable.
- **Fire follows the body.** Flames, embers, smoke, char and crackling follow
  each body's drawn pose, including falling ragdolls and blast fragments.
  Extinguished bodies steam. A burning player's view and held item show flames.
  People and animals also avoid walking into torches and campfires.
- **More reproducible QA.** Species dismemberment and body-fire capture scenes
  cover flight, rain, shelter, panic, death and the player. The F3 overlay and
  smoke report include burning and panicking bodies, ragdolls and pieces.

A Survival player inside their own scrap bomb's 3.9-block lethal radius or
powder keg's 5.7-block radius now dies outright, even behind cover.

## Save compatibility

The save format remains binary v3. Existing saves load in 0.8.1. Settled pieces
of every species and animal harvest records use the new optional `world.remains`
section. The 0.8.0 `world.fragments` section is still written unchanged, so 0.8.0
can open these saves while omitting animal pieces. This downgrade behavior is
reasoned from the older reader; running a 0.8.0 binary against a new save is not
part of the release validation. The older 0.7.2/0.7.3 restriction on saves with
the newer `world.bodies` section still applies.

Body fires, panic, scorch, burning liquid and block fires are transient and are
not restored on load. Burning bodies do not spread fire. Only lethal blasts
leave the player's remains; a player who burns to death leaves no body.

## Validation and packages

On 2026-09-28, `gradlew.bat releaseArtifacts --rerun-tasks --no-daemon
--console=plain` passed on Windows 11 Pro x64 with Temurin 25.0.4.1+1 and a
Ryzen 5 5600X: **1523 tests in 143 classes, no failures, errors or skipped tests**.
This includes Javadoc/doclint, the fat JAR and the Windows app-image ZIP. The
archive was checked for its executable, bundled runtime, versioned JAR, license,
Windows natives and required launcher options.

Seven native scenes produced 20 captures at 1280 x 720 with zero GL and KHR
errors: `dismember_species_wall`, `body_fire_row`, `body_fire_out`,
`body_fire_blast`, `body_fire_panic`, `body_fire_bird` and `body_fire_player`.
Representative captures were inspected for species anatomy, attached flames,
rain shelter, burning fragments, panic and birds in flight. The additional
release smoke run was stopped before its final report and is not counted as a
pass. The earlier completed development smoke and broader capture matrix are
recorded separately in the engineering record below.

`gradlew.bat performanceTest --no-daemon --console=plain` passed nine of ten
tests and both isolated rain tests. The durable-save gate failed at 5.872 ms
against 2.20 ms; load timing was not reached. Full-load combustion measured
0.1043 ms against 0.20 ms, fragment stepping 0.0333 ms against 1.00 ms, and
body-fire presentation 0.1019 ms per frame against 0.50 ms. These are secondary
host measurements, not a new calibration. No budget was relaxed, and this
release run did not repeat an older-version baseline to attribute the save
failure. See the earlier investigations in
[performance benchmarks](https://github.com/BehicKlncky/VEYLON-Deep-Frontier/blob/v0.8.1/docs/PERFORMANCE_BENCHMARKS.md).

The tag workflow builds and validates Windows x64, macOS Apple Silicon and
macOS Intel archives, then publishes them with `SHA256SUMS.txt`. Each archive
includes Java. macOS builds are unsigned and not notarized; macOS packaging is
validated by CI rather than by the local Windows checks.

See the [engineering record](https://github.com/BehicKlncky/VEYLON-Deep-Frontier/blob/v0.8.1/docs/engineering/ALL_LIVING_COMBAT_FIRE.md)
for the implementation contract, earlier development validation and known limits.
