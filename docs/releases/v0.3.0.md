## VEYLON: Deep Frontier 0.3.0

The world-expansion release: persistent procedural settlements and hostile
factions, siege and capture gameplay, three-zone deep caves, ranged combat,
black-powder weapons and explosives — all procedural, all deterministic per
seed.

### Downloads

| Platform | Asset |
| --- | --- |
| Windows 10/11 x64 | `veylon-0.3.0-windows-x64.zip` |
| macOS Apple Silicon | `veylon-0.3.0-macos-arm64.zip` |
| macOS Intel | `veylon-0.3.0-macos-x64.zip` |

Extract the matching archive, then launch `Veylon.exe` on Windows or
`Veylon.app` on macOS. A Java installation is not required for these packages.

The macOS applications are not yet Developer ID signed or notarized. Gatekeeper
may require explicit first-launch approval from **System Settings → Privacy &
Security**. Verify the downloaded archive against `SHA256SUMS.txt` before using
that override.

### Highlights

- **Procedural settlements**: deterministic regional planning — camps, common
  villages, uncommon forts, rare castles and very rare fortresses — with
  biome-adapted modular construction, a guaranteed friendly starter area and
  full map/discovery integration.
- **Living communities**: every settled human has a home, an archetype, a bed
  and a duty; residents sleep, work, patrol, trade, eat from real stocks, get
  sick, raise alarms and defend their walls, with bounded dormant simulation
  while you are away.
- **Headhunter faction & sieges**: trackers, scouts, hunters, brutes,
  powdermen and leaders; staged fortress assaults with gate, concealed postern,
  alarm suppression, captive rescue, powder-keg breach and patrol attrition;
  clear, occupy and then defend outposts against full-lifecycle counterattacks.
- **Targeted quests**: eleven settlement quest categories with concrete,
  persisted targets — the right settlement, party, captive, cave or outpost —
  acquired and turned in through provider dialogue.
- **Deep caves 2.0**: depth-relative root caves, basalt depths (sulfur,
  saltpeter, glow fungus, smoking fumarole vents) and rare resonant depths;
  guaranteed ladder-shaft cave mouths, reachable underground POIs and
  gloomstalker nest/ambush ecology.
- **Ranged combat & explosives**: bow with draw/release, ammo choice and arrow
  recovery; black-powder musket, flintlock and blunderbuss; powder kegs, scrap
  bombs and fire bombs on a reusable, occlusion- and resistance-aware explosion
  system with batched world edits; loot-only relic firearms.
- **Cave gear**: climbable rope ladders, refuelable charcoal lanterns with
  persisted finite fuel, and cheap emissive trail markers.
- **Determinism & QA**: AI decision jitter reseeds from the world seed, a
  160-test deterministic suite, runtime boundedness snapshots and a scripted
  fortress-approach performance gate in the 30-second smoke run.

### Compatibility

- Save format is now **binary v3**. v0.2.0 (v2) saves load through an explicit
  migration path and keep their exact legacy terrain — settlements, deep caves
  and the new resources appear only in worlds created on 0.3.0.
- Serialized `BlockType`/`ItemType` order is unchanged and append-only;
  ordinals from v0.1.0 remain valid. Prototype v1 saves are not supported.
- OpenGL 3.3 Core is required. All new art and audio is generated procedurally
  in code; no external assets were added.

See `CHANGELOG.md` in the repository for the full change list and known
limitations.
